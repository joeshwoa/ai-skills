# Contexts — the same topic is a different course

## School subject content (e.g. Don Bosco / technical secondary schools in Egypt)
- Learners 12–18; mixed levels; short attention spans → activity change every 10–15 min, lots of
  practice in the lab/workshop, competitions, visible progress.
- Follow the school/ministry curriculum and term calendar when given (ask for the official unit
  list, periods per week, exam dates, grading split: classwork / practical / final).
- Language: Egyptian Arabic explanations are fine and clearer for students; terms in English as
  written in the code/tools; formal Arabic for documents sent to administration if they ask.
- Deliverables most used: lesson plans (for the supervisor), slides, worksheets, practical lab
  sheets, quizzes + keys, gradebook, attendance. Keep printing in mind (A4, black-and-white safe).
- Safety rules for labs/workshops; parent communication if relevant; age-appropriate examples.
- Technical schools: tie every concept to the trade (electronics, mechanics, IT) and to real jobs.

## University course
- Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) mapped to Programme Learning Outcomes (PLOs); credit hours
  (1 credit ≈ 1 lecture hour/week or 2–3 lab hours); syllabus with policies and references.
- Accreditation-friendly: alignment matrix, assessment weights, rubrics, course file evidence.
- Lecture + section/lab split; midterm + final + coursework; academic integrity statement.

## Academy / bootcamp / training centre programme (e.g. Neo AI, LTC, Masartech, Coach Academy)
- Outcome = a job-ready skill or portfolio: project-based, each session ends with a working piece.
- Programme = several courses → one plan per course + a programme map (prerequisites, tracks).
- Certificates (attendance vs. completion vs. assessed), capstone with rubric, demo day.
- Market-current tools and versions; career elements (CV, GitHub, interviews) where relevant.
- Pricing/marketing material may be needed → premium-documents (one-pager/brochure), reels-studio.

## Corporate training
- Start from the business problem and the job tasks; outcomes as on-the-job behaviours.
- Short modules (60–90 min), case studies from their industry, job aids/checklists.
- Evaluation: Kirkpatrick levels (reaction, learning, behaviour, results) — include a pre/post quiz
  and a follow-up action plan.

## Online / self-paced
- Micro-lessons 5–12 min, each with a check; downloadable handouts; clear progress path;
  discussion prompts; auto-graded quizzes (question bank) + one assessed project.
- Video scripts → reels-studio (long-form lesson scripts) and video-post-studio.

## Single workshop
- One outcome, one artefact by the end, 60–80% hands-on; pre-work list; take-home handout.
