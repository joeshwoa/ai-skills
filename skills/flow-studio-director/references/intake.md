# Intake — the question bank

Never assume a detail that changes the output. Ask as **numbered
multiple-choice**, one **RECOMMENDED** option each, always a last "you
decide". Max 6 questions per message, answerable in one line
("1b 2a 3c…"). Pre-fill anything already known and don't ask it.

Escape hatch: "just go" / "all recommended" / "كمّل" → take every
recommended option and print one compact line of the assumptions made.

## Round 1 — the job (always)

1. **Deliverable & platform** — a) Reels/TikTok/Shorts 9:16 b) YouTube 16:9
   c) ad (all ratios) d) cinematic short e) explainer f) you decide
2. **Length** — a) 8 s b) 15–30 s (RECOMMENDED for social) c) 30–60 s d) 60 s+
3. **Model preference** — a) route per shot, cheapest that does the job
   well (RECOMMENDED) b) best quality regardless c) Omni only d) Veo only
   e) cheapest possible f) I'll name one
4. **How hands-on should I be?** — a) per shot: I generate, check, show
   you, wait (RECOMMENDED, safest) b) I ask before every generation
   c) autopilot with a credit budget — I run it all and stop at the limit
   d) guided — I write everything, you click Generate
5. **Dialogue language** — a) Egyptian Arabic b) MSA c) Gulf d) Levantine
   e) English f) bilingual g) no dialogue
6. **Budget** — a) ≤50 credits b) ≤150 c) ≤400 d) a number e) whatever it
   takes — show me the estimate first (RECOMMENDED)

## Round 2 — the look (skip what the brief already fixes)

1. **Style** — a) photoreal cinematic b) 3D animated (Pixar-like)
   c) documentary d) commercial/product e) anime/stylised f) retro film
2. **Lens & camera** — a) mixed per shot (RECOMMENDED) b) wide 24 mm
   c) natural 35–50 mm d) portrait 85 mm e) anamorphic
3. **Light & tone** — a) golden hour b) bright high-key c) low-key moody
   d) neon night e) overcast soft f) per scene (RECOMMENDED)
4. **Voice-over narrator?** — a) none, characters only (RECOMMENDED if
   there are characters) b) Gemini TTS (free, same voices as Flow)
   c) ElevenLabs (natural; free tier is non-commercial) d) I'll record it
5. **Music** — a) added in post, I'll brief it (RECOMMENDED) b) none
   c) the user supplies a track
6. **Assets you have** — logo, product photos, character refs, existing
   Flow Characters/Voices, footage?

## Mid-build micro-choices

Small decisions that come up while building (which hand holds the cup,
wall colour, how long to hold the end frame) go in a **MICRO-CHOICES**
block at the end of that deliverable with a recommended pick each, so
the user can confirm them all at once.

## Never invent

Facts about the user's brand, product, people, prices, claims, or
footage. If it's missing, ask; if it's unknowable, leave a visible
`[TO CONFIRM]` rather than a plausible guess.
