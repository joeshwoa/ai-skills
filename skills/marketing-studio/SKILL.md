# Marketing Studio

The strategy layer above the channel skills. It decides what to say, to whom, through which channels and how success is measured; then it hands precise briefs to the execution skills.

## Workflow

1. **Diagnose**: business model, unit economics, current customers, current channels, constraints (budget, team, legal), time horizon. Ask at most 5 questions, then proceed with stated assumptions.
2. **Research** (use live search for market facts, cite sources and dates; label anything unsourced as an assumption).
3. **Position**: audience segments, the job they hire the product for, alternatives they use today, the one claim you can prove, proof assets.
4. **Choose channels** by where the segment already pays attention and what the economics allow, not by what is fashionable. Write down what you are deliberately NOT doing.
5. **Plan**: 30/60/90 days with owners, budget, deliverables and the measurement for each. Include a weekly operating rhythm.
6. **Hand off**: write the brief for each execution skill (see below) so no information is re-asked.
7. **Measure and adjust**: define leading and lagging KPIs and the review date before launching.

## Hard rules

- Never invent market sizes, conversion rates or competitor facts. Cite or label as an assumption to verify.
- Strategy must be falsifiable: every plan has a metric, a threshold and a date to decide continue / change / stop.
- No deceptive tactics: fake scarcity, fake testimonials, undisclosed endorsements, misleading before/after, unsolicited bulk messaging.
- Respect privacy and consent rules for email and WhatsApp (opt-in, easy opt-out); not legal advice, flag where a lawyer is needed.
- Arabic work is written natively in the target dialect, not translated from English.

## Output formats

One-page strategy; 30/60/90 plan table; messaging house (promise, 3 pillars, proof, tone); channel scorecard; launch timeline; KPI tree; competitor matrix. Templates in `templates/`.
