# Document types — what each must contain (outline before writing)

Each entry: purpose · must-have sections (in order) · blocks that work · length · watch-outs.

## Business
**report** — inform a decision. Cover (photo) · contents · executive summary (lead + stats + the
recommendation in a callout) · context/method · findings (one heading per finding: claim → chart or
table → so-what) · recommendations (steps/cards) · next steps · appendix. 6–40 pp. Every chart has
a caption that states the takeaway.

**whitepaper** — teach + persuade. Problem · why now · approach · evidence · how to adopt · about.
Art cover, pull quotes, definitions, numbered sections. 8–20 pp.

**proposal** — win approval. Split cover · summary (what, for whom, outcome, cost) · objectives ·
approach (steps) · timeline/plan (table or timeline) · team · budget (table with total_row + chart)
· why us (cards) · terms · signature. Tie every cost to a deliverable.

**business_plan** — company, problem, solution, market (numbers + sources), model, go-to-market,
competition (table), operations, team, financials (tables from premium-spreadsheets), risks, ask.

**case_study** — client, challenge, solution, results (stats first!), quote, what's next. 2–4 pp.

**one_pager / flyer** — one message, hero image, 3 benefits (cards), proof (stat), CTA band.
**brochure** — cover photo, about, services (cards), process (steps), proof, contact.
**newsletter** — masthead, 3–5 stories (heading + paragraph + image), dates, contacts; 2 columns.

## Operations & legal
**manual / guide / ebook** — how-to: contents, numbered sections or chapters (`section` blocks),
steps with images/code, callouts for warnings, troubleshooting table, glossary (definitions).
**policy / sop** — purpose, scope, definitions, responsibilities (table), procedure (numbered
steps), records, review date/version. Numbered headings.
**contract / nda** — parties (kv), recitals, definitions, numbered clauses (scope, term, payment,
confidentiality, IP, liability, termination, governing law), signatures. Always: "draft — have a
lawyer review"; take every fact (names, amounts, dates, jurisdiction) from the user.
**minutes** — meta (date, attendees), agenda items, decisions (callout success), action items
(table: action · owner · due), next meeting.
**memo** — to/from/date/subject, bottom line first, background, request, deadline.

## Letters & personal
**letter / cover_letter** — letterhead, date, recipient, subject, greeting, 3–4 short paragraphs
(why you/this, proof, ask), closing, signature. Cover letter: mirror the job post's needs with one
proof each; ≤ 1 page.
**cv** — name + role, profile (2–3 lines), experience (role, org, dates, 2–4 achievement bullets
with numbers), education, skills (grouped), languages (levels), projects/certs. ATS-safe: real
text, standard headings. The ML/AI vs Flutter emphasis follows the target job.

## Money
**invoice / quotation / receipt** — `items` block computes totals; meta number/date/due; bill-to
complete; payment details (kv); terms. Quotation: validity date + scope notes. Never hand-type totals.

## Education
**lesson_plan** — meta (subject, grade, duration), objectives (measurable verbs), materials,
schedule block (time · phase · teacher · students), differentiation, assessment, homework.
**syllabus** — description, outcomes, schedule, assessment weights, policies, contacts.
**course_plan** — see course-studio.
**handout** — title band, objectives box, explanation with definitions/examples/code, summary,
practice. **worksheet / lab_sheet** — name/class/date fields, instructions, questions with points
and space, challenge. **exam** — header with duration and total, instructions, sections by type,
points per question; **answer_key** separate with explanations.
**certificate** — landscape; recipient name largest; reason specific (course + hours + grade);
date, signatures, seal.
**instructor_guide** — per session: timing table, what to say/do, misconceptions, answers.

## Writing quality bar (all types)
- Bottom line first. Headings that say something ("Prep time fell 40%" not "Results").
- Short paragraphs (≤ 4 lines), concrete nouns, active verbs, numbers with units and sources.
- Arabic: one register throughout (MSA for formal/legal/government; Egyptian for students,
  social, friendly brands); English technical terms where that is how people say them.
- Proofread names, numbers, dates twice. Leave nothing generic: if a sentence fits any company,
  rewrite it for this one.
