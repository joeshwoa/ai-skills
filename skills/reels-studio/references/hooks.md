# Hooks — the first 3 seconds

Most viewers decide in 1–3 s. A hook is three layers that land **together**:
1. **Visual hook** — the first frame moves or surprises (motion, a strange object, a face very
   close, a result shown first, a glitch, text appearing, a hand slamming something on the desk).
2. **Spoken hook** — ≤ ~12 words (≈ 3 s), says who it's for or why to stay.
3. **On-screen text** — 3–7 words, readable muted, in the safe zone; it doesn't have to repeat the
   spoken line word for word — it can add the stakes.

Rules: no greetings or intros ("Hi guys, today…", "أهلاً بيكم في فيديو جديد"); no logo first; start
mid-action; promise something the video actually delivers in the end (an honest open loop);
speak to one person ("you"/"إنت").

## Hooks by content type
- **Reels**: everything above, ≤ 3 s.
- **Ads**: same 3 s rule, but the brand/product must register in the first 5 s (logo bug, product in
  frame, or the name said) — skippers should still know who it was. Lead with the viewer's problem or
  the result, not the company. 3–5 hook variants recorded as separate openings for testing.
- **Long-form**: a cold open (the most striking moment, ≤ 15 s) then the promise (what they'll get,
  ≤ 45 s total). Confirm the title/thumbnail promise immediately. Never open with "welcome back",
  channel intros or sponsor reads.

## Visual hook menu
Push-in from wide to close in 1 s · whip pan · object drops into frame · talent mid-sentence as
the cut starts · split screen A vs B · screen recording with the result already on screen ·
text typing itself · glitch/brand transition (Neo AI trend reels: Talent 0.4 s → glitch → mascot
0.4 s → glitch → talent) · a sound effect + freeze frame · hands doing something satisfying.

## Retention after the hook
- **Open a loop** in the hook, close it at the end (or near it).
- **Re-hook** around the middle: "But here's the part nobody tells you…" / "بس الأهم بقى…".
- **Pattern change every 2–4 s**: angle, zoom, B-roll, text pop, sound — not every second.
- **Payoff before the CTA**, never instead of it.
- **Loop ending**: last line flows into the first ("…and that's why—" → "Stop paying for…").

## Scoring a hook (0–5 each, keep ≥ 3 on all)
Clarity (gets it in one listen) · Relevance (persona feels "that's me") · Curiosity (needs the
next line) · Credibility (not clickbait the video can't pay off) · Speed (≤ 3 s) · Visual (works muted).
