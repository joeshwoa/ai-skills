# Platforms — specs, signals, habits

**Platforms change limits and ranking often. The numbers below were current as of mid-2026 —
re-check the platform's own help/creator pages before relying on a limit, and say "verify" when
you quote one to the user.** Hard limits the validator enforces are in `scripts/validate_script.py`.

## Shared vertical spec
1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps (or native), H.264/H.265 + AAC, sound designed for both on and muted
(captions always). Cover/thumbnail 1080×1920 with the key words inside the centre 1080×1350/1440.

**Safe zones (1080×1920):** keep text and faces out of the top ~220 px (account name, close button)
and the bottom ~380–450 px (caption, sound, CTA buttons — TikTok's is the tallest), and ~120 px
from the right edge (like/comment/share column). Centre band ≈ y 250–1450 is safe everywhere.

## Instagram Reels
- Length: up to 3 min for Reels (longer clips post as Reels too since 2025); 15–60 s is still the
  sweet spot for reach; under ~90 s is what Instagram recommends to non-followers most.
- Signals Instagram has publicly named: **watch time**, **likes per reach**, **sends (shares in DMs)
  per reach** — sends matter most for reaching new people. Saves and comments help.
- Original content is favoured; reposts/aggregated content and visible watermarks from other apps
  are down-ranked.
- Profile grid shows covers at 3:4 — keep cover text centred.
- Caption ≤ 2,200 chars; hashtags: Instagram limited posts to **5 hashtags** (late 2025 — verify);
  use 3–5 specific ones; keywords in the caption and on-screen text help Instagram search.
- **Trial Reels**: shown first to non-followers — use for A/B testing hooks.
- Music: business accounts get a restricted commercial library; original audio is safest.

## TikTok
- Length: uploads up to 10 min in-app (60 min on some accounts/desktop); the Creator Rewards
  Program pays only for videos over 1 min. Short (15–45 s) still loops best.
- Signals: completion and re-watches, shares, comments, follows from the video, searches.
- **TikTok is a search engine** for under-30s: say the keyword out loud, put it on screen and in
  the caption (captions up to 4,000 chars).
- Trends: Creative Center (by country), sounds with rising use, stitches/duets.
- Business accounts: Commercial Music Library only.

## YouTube Shorts
- Length: up to 3 min (since Oct 2024); square or vertical.
- Title (≤ 100 chars) matters for search; description; up to 3 hashtags show above the title.
- "Related video" link drives viewers to a long video — use it.
- Signals: viewed vs swiped away, average view duration, re-watches, likes, subscribers gained.
- Loops count: endings that flow into the start lift retention.

## Facebook Reels
- Meta treats all new videos as reels on Facebook (2025). Original content favoured; reposted
  content loses distribution. Captions and the first 3 s matter as on Instagram.
- Audience skews older in Egypt than Instagram/TikTok — adjust humour and references.
- Cross-post from Instagram is fine, but check the caption reads well on Facebook.

## LinkedIn video
- Vertical video feed exists; professional audiences, mostly muted viewing → burned captions.
- Hook = a professional pain or a number; keep it 30–90 s; no dance trends.
- Caption up to 3,000 chars; 3–5 hashtags at most.

## Snapchat Spotlight
- Vertical; length limits have changed several times (60 s historically — verify current); younger audience; trending sounds/Lenses; caption very short (≤ 160 chars).

## YouTube long-form
- 16:9 (1920×1080 or 4K), chapters (first at 00:00, ≥ 3, each ≥ 10 s), title ≤ 100 chars (keep the
  key part in ~60), description 5,000 chars, custom thumbnail 1280×720 (< 2 MB), end screen 5–20 s,
  cards, captions/subtitles uploaded as .srt.
- Signals YouTube talks about: click-through on the thumbnail, average view duration and
  percentage viewed, returning viewers, satisfaction surveys. A strong first 30 s protects the CTR you earned.
- Vertical clips from the long video → Shorts (link back with "related video").

## Posting habits (general — the account's own Insights beat any rule)
- Consistency beats volume: 3–5 reels/week for a growing account is common practice.
- Post when the audience is on: in Egypt, evenings (after work, ~7–11 pm Cairo) are typical; in
  Ramadan, after iftar and late night. Confirm with Insights.
- Reply to comments in the first hour (pin a good one; reply with a video if useful).
- Re-use a winner: re-cut with a new hook rather than reposting the same file.
