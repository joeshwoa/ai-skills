# Review checklist — the independent reviewer

Run after `validate_script.py` passes. Write findings to `<project>/review.md` as
`[must-fix | should-fix | ok] finding → fix`. The writer fixes; the user accepts anything left.

## 1. Does it work as a video?
- [ ] Would the persona stop at the first frame and the first line? (read it aloud, picture the feed)
- [ ] One clear idea; every scene earns its seconds; nothing repeats without adding.
- [ ] A payoff that matches the hook's promise (no bait-and-switch).
- [ ] Re-hook or pattern change keeps the middle alive.
- [ ] CTA = one action, fits the goal and awareness stage, said and shown.
- [ ] Works muted (on-screen text + captions) and with sound.

## 2. Audience & language
- [ ] Every line sounds like the audience's dialect and register (native read-through recommended
      for any variety the writer isn't strong in).
- [ ] English terms mixed the way this audience mixes them — consistent spelling throughout.
- [ ] Cultural fit: humour, references, religion, gender and family norms of the market; occasion-aware
      (e.g. Ramadan tone).

## 3. Truth, claims, consent
- [ ] Every fact, number, price, date, result is from the user or a cited source. No invented stats.
- [ ] No promises the product can't keep ("guaranteed", "100%", income claims) — soften or prove.
- [ ] Testimonials/UGC only from real people, their real words, with written consent; results typical
      or labelled.
- [ ] No impersonation of real people/brands; no fake screenshots, reviews or "news".
- [ ] Comparisons with competitors are fair and verifiable (or don't name them).
- [ ] Paid partnerships/sponsorships disclosed (platform's paid-partnership label).
- [ ] Health, finance, legal, education-outcome claims: extra care; add disclaimers where needed.

## 4. Rights
- [ ] Music: original, licensed, or the platform's commercial library for business accounts.
- [ ] Footage/images: own, licensed, or generated with rights to use; no other creators' clips
      except via stitch/duet features.
- [ ] Logos/trademarks of others only in fair, factual reference.
- [ ] People recognisable in frame have agreed (minors: guardian consent; avoid featuring minors
      unless essential).

## 5. Platform policy
- [ ] No engagement bait, no misleading thumbnails, no shock for shock's sake.
- [ ] Nothing in the safe-zone danger areas; captions readable.
- [ ] Sensitive topics (politics, religion, tragedies) avoided or handled with care per brand rules.

## 5b. Ads only
- [ ] Brand/product registers in the first 5 s; the CTA is said and shown; the end card matches the landing page.
- [ ] No personal attributes asserted about the viewer (health, weight, finances, religion, ethnicity,
      sexuality, age…); no "before/after" in restricted categories.
- [ ] Every claim in the video, supers and ad copy is provable on the landing page; offer terms and
      legal supers exact.
- [ ] Restricted categories (health, finance, employment, housing, education outcomes, politics) handled
      per current policy (special ad category, disclaimers) — or flagged to the user.
- [ ] Each cutdown makes sense alone (own hook, own CTA) and fits its slot.
- [ ] Creator/UGC ads disclosed as paid partnerships.

## 5c. Long-form only
- [ ] The first 30 s deliver what the title and thumbnail promise.
- [ ] Every fact has a source in the researcher's notes; quotes are exact and attributed.
- [ ] Chapters make sense as a table of contents; a re-hook opens each.
- [ ] Guests/interviewees agreed to be recorded and published.

## 6. Brand
- [ ] Voice matches the brand file (tone words, banned words/visuals).
- [ ] End frame and CTA wording consistent with the rest of the campaign.
- [ ] Document style matches the brand's scripts (colours, labels, scene naming).

## 7. Production readiness
- [ ] Editor could cut it from the blue lines alone; talent could perform it from the red lines alone.
- [ ] Runtime realistic (read it out with a timer for anything over 45 s).
- [ ] Props, locations, B-roll and screens listed and obtainable.
