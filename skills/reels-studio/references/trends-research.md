# Trend research — live, dated, honest

Trends live 3 days to 3 weeks. The rule: **anything presented as "trending now" was checked
today, with a source and a date.** If a source can't be reached, say "unverified" and offer an
evergreen alternative. Never present remembered trends as current.

## Where to look (in this order)
1. **TikTok Creative Center** (ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter) → Trends: hashtags, songs,
   creators, top videos; filter by country (Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE… when listed) and 7-day window.
   Songs list shows which are approved for business use.
2. **Instagram** — Reels tab and audio pages: a ↗ arrow next to an audio = trending; the audio page
   shows how many reels use it (rising fast + under ~50k uses = early). Instagram's
   @creators account and "Trends" posts.
3. **YouTube** — Shorts feed + YouTube's own trends/Culture & Trends reports; search suggestions.
4. **Google Trends** (trends.google.com) → country, past 7 days, "rising" queries; also the
   "Trending now" page for the country.
5. **X/Twitter trends** for the country (news, football, memes, controversies — handle with care).
6. **News & industry** for news-jacking (e.g. a new AI model launch): official announcements first,
   then coverage. Date of the event matters more than date of the post.
7. **Competitors & creators the audience follows** — their last 10 reels sorted by views vs their
   average: an outlier (3×+ their usual) shows a format that works for this audience right now.
8. **The comments** under those outliers — the audience's exact words and questions.

Use the web-search / browser tools available. If none are available, say so, use evergreen
formats, and ask the user to paste 2–3 reels they've seen trending.

## Log format (`trends.md`)
```
| Trend | Type | Where seen | Source link | Checked | Stage | Fit (0–5) | How we'd use it | Rights |
| "POV: …" format | format | TikTok EG | <url> | 2026-10-03 | peaking | 4 | … | n/a |
| <song name> | audio | IG Reels | <url> | 2026-10-03 | emerging | 3 | bg under VO | business? check library |
```
Copy the trends you actually use into `spec.brief.trend_refs` with `checked` dates — the validator
warns when one is older than 14 days.

## Lifecycle — when to jump
- **Emerging** (a few creators, rising fast): best upside; jump within 24–72 h if it fits.
- **Peaking** (everyone's doing it): still works if your twist is clearly yours.
- **Dying** (brands and parody accounts on it): skip, or only as self-aware humour.
- A trend that doesn't fit the brand or the persona is skipped, no matter how big.

## Trend types
- **Audio**: a sound people make videos to. Business accounts: use the platform's commercial
  library (TikTok Commercial Music Library, Meta Sound Collection) or original audio — popular
  songs are often not licensed for business use and can be muted or removed.
- **Format/template**: POV, "things I wish I knew", "tell me without telling me", green screen,
  "day in the life", "rate my…", before/after transitions, "if X were a person", "a thing I'd
  never do as a <job>", "unpopular opinion", "3 signs…", "this vs that".
- **Meme/phrase**: a line everyone's quoting (local memes travel fastest in Egypt — check meaning
  and origin before using; some are tied to politics, religion or a person's tragedy).
- **News-jacking**: a launch, an event, a match, a viral moment — react fast with *your* angle
  (the Neo AI "new model every week" reel is this type).
- **Seasonal/occasion**: see calendar below — plan 1–2 weeks ahead.
- **Platform feature**: new effects, templates, collab posts, trial reels — early users often get a boost.

## Adapting a trend (keep the recognisable part, change the meaning)
1. Name the trend's mechanic (the beat, the structure, the punchline shape).
2. Keep that mechanic + the sound/format cue so people recognise it in 1 s.
3. Replace the content with the persona's world and the brand's point.
4. Land on the brand's message without killing the joke (brand appears as the twist or the fix).
5. Ask: would this audience share it with a friend? If not, it's an ad wearing a costume.

## Evergreen formats that work without a trend
Myth vs fact · mistakes to avoid · "how I would X if I started today" · before/after ·
step-by-step in 30 s · hot take + reason · story with a twist · reaction/duet/stitch to a real
post · FAQ answer (from real comments) · behind the scenes · comparison (cheap vs expensive,
old way vs new way) · challenge · series "Day 1 of…".

## Red lines
No tragedies, deaths, disasters or religious/political controversies as "trends" for brand
content. No using a real person's likeness/voice without permission. No copying another creator's
video beat-for-beat — adapt the format, credit when stitching/dueting.
