# Compliance and ethics
Purpose: principles and checks for disclosure, giveaways, copyright, privacy, minors, sensitive topics, AI labelling, accessibility and brand safety, ending with a 15-point release gate.
Last updated: 2026-10-19

Not legal advice. Where this file states a law, it says whether it was read and where. Items marked `unverified` must be confirmed by counsel or the official text before use. Platform rules live in `platforms/<slug>.md` (section 6 policy, section 7 disclosure); this file does not copy them.

## 1. Principles
1. Honest: no fake reviews, no sock puppets, no bought engagement, no undisclosed paid content (SKILL.md hard rules).
2. Consent first: people's data, images, words and messages are used only with a clear reason and permission.
3. Least data: collect and keep the minimum, for the shortest time.
4. Proof before claims: any result, health, finance or "best" claim needs evidence on file.
5. Official channels only: official APIs and scheduling tools, no scraping behind logins.
6. A named human is accountable for each campaign and approves releases.
7. When rules conflict, follow the stricter one (platform, law, brand).

## 2. Ad and sponsorship disclosure
Principles (consistent across the FTC guidance cited in the platform files): disclose any material connection (payment, gift, free product, affiliate link, employment, family) clearly, early, in plain words, in the same language as the endorsement; in video disclose in the video itself, not only in the caption; a platform label alone may not be enough; the endorser and the brand are both responsible. Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers (cited in `platforms/instagram.md`, `platforms/facebook.md`; not re-fetched this session).
Wording: "Ad" / "Sponsored" / "Paid partnership"; Arabic "إعلان" or "برعاية" at the start of the copy (`platforms/facebook.md` section 7 practical rule).

## 3. Influencer relations basics
- Contract: scope, deliverables, usage rights and duration, exclusivity, payment, approval process, disclosure duty, takedown clause, rights to boost with paid ads (partnership ads), data handling, termination, no fake engagement clause.
- Brief: message, must-say and must-not-say, claims you can prove, disclosure wording and location, sensitive-topic list, deadline, review window (24 to 48 h heuristic).
- Vetting: audience fit, authenticity (comment quality, not follower count), past brand conflicts, history of undisclosed ads, age of audience (children: section 7).
- Review before posting; check the disclosure is in the video/caption and the label is on.
- Gifts count as a material connection. Do not require positive reviews. Do not ask for engagement pods.
- Egypt/MENA norms: `egypt-mena-playbook.md` section 8.
- Pay and record: keep invoices and tax details per local law (`unverified` for Egyptian influencer tax/licensing rules).

## 4. Giveaways and contests
Platform rules: read `platforms/<slug>.md` section 6 and the giveaway case in section 10; most platform policy pages could not be read, so the files list conservative rules. Common points they state: no required mass-tagging, no like-for-like, no sub-gating (YouTube), one entry method (X), no saves-for-entry (Pinterest), statement that the platform does not sponsor (Instagram), form or event registration instead of "like + comment + tag" (LinkedIn).
General legal flags (not legal advice):
- Written official rules on a page: eligibility, region, dates, prize and value, how winners are chosen, how winners are told and when, who is the organiser, data use, cost of entry (none, preferably).
- Skill vs chance: random draws, purchases required, and paid entry may turn a promotion into a regulated lottery in some countries. Egyptian rules for prize promotions, lotteries and any permit requirements: `unverified`; ask counsel before any promotion that requires a purchase or collects entry fees.
- Data: entrants' data only for the contest unless consent says otherwise (section 6).
- Minors: exclude under-18s unless a guardian route is legal and documented.
- Prize delivery proof, taxes, and publishing winners (with permission).
- Sponsor and platform statements; disclose partner brands.
- Fraud checks by hand; do not use bots to pick or detect winners without a documented method.

## 5. Copyright, music and UGC
- Own or license everything: images, fonts, video, music, voice, templates. "Found on the internet" is not a licence. Keep licence proof with the post pack.
- Music: platform music libraries are often licensed for personal or creator accounts, not for business promotion; limits differ per platform (Instagram library limits `unverified`, `platforms/instagram.md` section 6; TikTok Commercial Music Library, `platforms/tiktok.md`). When in doubt use licensed or original audio.
- Claims and takedowns: handle through the platform's dispute path (`platforms/youtube.md` section 6 timelines, `platforms/telegram.md` copyright case, `platforms/instagram.md` case 3). Do not re-upload the same file repeatedly.
- UGC and customer content: ask permission in writing (DM reply counts if saved) for each reuse, state where it will appear, credit the creator, allow withdrawal. Template 16 in `community-management.md`.
- People in photos and video: written consent from identifiable people; extra care for staff and customers; no faces of children without a guardian's written consent.
- Reviews and testimonials: real, current, with permission; do not edit meaning.
- Competitor content: no copying, no impersonation, comparative claims only with proof.
- Stock and AI assets: check the licence allows commercial social use; keep receipts (`stock-library` skill when installed).

## 6. Privacy and consent
Egypt (from the DLA Piper summary of Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020, last modified 2026-02-13; secondary source, read 2026-10-19):
- Processing needs explicit consent of the data subject unless the law allows another basis (Art. 2, Art. 6: contract, legal obligation, court ruling, legitimate interest).
- Electronic direct marketing: consent, sender identity, valid contact address, clear marketing purpose and an easy opt-out (Art. 17); keep consent records for 3 years after the last message (Art. 18); a Personal Data Protection Centre licence or permit is needed for electronic marketing activities.
- Cross-border transfers have conditions (Art. 14 to 15). If your tools (CRM, n8n or Make cloud, LLM APIs) process Egyptian personal data abroad, ask counsel whether a transfer condition applies.
- Breach: notify the Centre within 72 hours and data subjects within 3 days of that notice (Art. 7, per DLA Piper).
- Children's data is sensitive data.
- Penalties summarised: fines up to EGP 5,000,000 and imprisonment possible; Anti-Cybercrime Law 175/2018 Art. 25 covers unauthorised marketing messages (fines EGP 50,000 to 100,000 and imprisonment, per DLA Piper).
- Executive Regulations: DLA Piper says they were "recently issued" without a date; the date, scope and current licensing practice are `unverified`. Law text and Centre guidance should be read before any consent system is finalised: https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=law&c=EG
EU/UK (GDPR, ePrivacy), California (CCPA), US messaging (TCPA-type consent): named in the platform files; not read this session: `unverified`. If the audience includes those regions apply the stricter rules.
Operational rules:
- Messaging opt-in: record source, time, wording shown, channel and category (`platforms/whatsapp.md` section 7). Opt-out honoured within the same day (heuristic) and logged.
- Data minimisation: no national ID, card, health or password data in chat; do not copy customer messages into prompts, logs, screenshots shared widely, or public reports unless needed and consented.
- LinkedIn and X API data rules limit storage and reuse (`platforms/linkedin.md` section 8, `platforms/x-twitter.md` section 8).
- Retention: define periods for leads (for example 12 months from last contact, heuristic), logs and screenshots; delete on schedule.
- Vendors: use data processing terms with schedulers, CRMs and LLM providers; never send secrets or personal data in prompts (`automation-recipes.md` section 7).
- Cookies/pixels on the landing page are a website matter: hand to `marketing-studio`.

## 7. Minors and sensitive categories
- Minors: respect platform minimum ages (13 on Instagram and TikTok per the platform files; teen restrictions vary); do not target, DM or feature minors for promotion without guardian consent; YouTube "made for kids" flag is legally driven (`platforms/youtube.md` section 7); advertising to children in Egypt/MENA: `egypt-mena-playbook.md` section 7; TikTok bans some categories for branded content (`platforms/tiktok.md` section 7).
- Health: no cures, "guaranteed" results, before/after medical claims, or advice replacing a clinician; supplements, cosmetics and medical devices have their own rules (`unverified` in Egypt; counsel or regulator check); disclaimers do not fix false claims.
- Finance: no promised returns or guarantees; risk statements where law requires; crypto, lending, trading and forex are restricted ad categories on several platforms (`platforms/tiktok.md` section 7, platform ad policies); local licensing `unverified`.
- Politics, elections, religion: brand posts avoid them unless it is the brand's explicit field; political ads and issue ads have platform rules (`unverified` in most platform files); keep staff personal posts separate; see `egypt-mena-playbook.md` section 7.
- Sensitive personal data (religion, health, sexual life, ethnicity, politics, biometrics): never collect in comments, DMs, forms or contests (`platforms/snapchat.md` section 7).
- Tragedies and disasters: pause promotional content; no trend-jacking.

## 8. AI-generated and AI-altered content
- Disclose realistic synthetic or altered media where the platform asks and use the platform control: TikTok `is_aigc` label (`platforms/tiktok.md` section 6), YouTube altered or synthetic content (`platforms/youtube.md` sections 6 and 7), Instagram `is_ai_generated` (`platforms/instagram.md` section 7), X `made_with_ai` (`platforms/x-twitter.md` section 7), Pinterest AI-modified toggle (`platforms/pinterest.md` section 6), Snapchat ranking note (`platforms/snapchat.md` section 5).
- Never use AI to fake a person's endorsement, voice or face without written consent; no synthetic customers, testimonials or reviews (`platforms/reddit.md`, `platforms/google-business-profile.md` for review rules).
- Human review of every AI draft; check facts, numbers, names and claims. Keep prompt and output with the post pack for the audit trail.
- AI chat replies: tell people they are talking to an automated assistant where the platform or law asks; offer a human (`platforms/instagram.md` section 6, `platforms/whatsapp.md` section 6 on AI-provider chatbots).
- AI-generated engagement (fake comments, AI "users") is deceptive and forbidden.

## 9. Accessibility
Alt text, captions, contrast, spelled-out numbers and readable Arabic: `content-craft.md` section 5. Accessible customer service: offer a text option for voice-only channels, reply to voice notes with text when asked, avoid colour-only signals, keep links descriptive.

## 10. Brand safety
- Keep a "never" list: topics, jokes, accounts and hashtags the brand will not touch; review quarterly.
- Check adjacency: comments under ads/posts, collaborations with creators, paid placements; mute keywords and use filters.
- Employee posting: guidelines for personal accounts mentioning the brand (disclose affiliation).
- Partner and influencer vetting (section 3). Review audio and visuals for hidden logos, license plates, phone numbers, children and private documents.
- Crisis readiness: `crisis-playbook.md`.

## 11. Release gate: 15 points (all must be yes)
1. Is the approver named and has approved this exact final text, media, time and link?
2. Is every fact, price, date, phone and link verified against the source of truth?
3. Do all claims (results, health, finance, "best", "first", "guaranteed") have evidence on file or are removed?
4. Is paid, gifted, affiliate or own-brand ad content disclosed with the platform tool and a plain word?
5. Are all assets licensed or original (images, fonts, music, templates, stock, AI tools)?
6. Do all identifiable people and customers have written consent, and are minors excluded or covered by guardian consent?
7. Is any personal data (names, numbers, orders, screenshots) removed or consented?
8. Is realistic AI-generated or altered media labelled with the platform's control?
9. Does the post respect the platform file's limits and the pre-publish checklist (section 12 of the platform file; `scripts/limits_check.py` run)?
10. Alt text, captions and contrast present and Arabic RTL preview checked?
11. Is the language, dialect and tone appropriate, and free of religious, political or sensitive-topic risks (`egypt-mena-playbook.md` section 7)?
12. For giveaways: official rules published, no purchase or tagging mandate, data use stated, counsel check where required?
13. For messages: recipients opted in, window/template rules respected, opt-out available, automation disclosed with human fallback?
14. Is tracking set (UTM via `scripts/utm.py`) and an owner assigned to answer comments and DMs after publishing?
15. Is the kill switch/rollback known (who can pause or delete, how fast) and is nothing tone-deaf scheduled for this period?
Any "no": do not publish; fix or escalate.
